# Stage 12.2E relevance annotation

Score each candidate independently for the stated tourist query.

- **0 — Irrelevant:** not a plausible answer.
- **1 — Weakly relevant:** related but a poor fit for interest, location, category, activity, or suitability.
- **2 — Relevant:** a reasonable recommendation.
- **3 — Highly relevant:** an excellent direct match.

Do not infer missing facts. Consider interests, geography, requested categories and activities, and actual tourist suitability. Reviewers R1 and R2 must score independently. Leave a score blank if unsure; do not copy another reviewer. Disagreements require a separate adjudication decision and notes.
