# Stage 12.5 Limitations and Next Steps

- The four-layer weights and evidence-quality coefficients are transparent research baselines, not calibrated optima.
- Weather quality depends on provider/model coverage and credentials.
- Long-range historical climatology is not scored as weather.
- Opening-hour absence remains unknown rather than open.
- Event evidence is used only when verified by the existing service.
- Unified attraction-ranking accuracy is not independently validated.
- Group emotional intelligence remains deferred.

The three-query real-data shadow pilot completed, but it is too small for performance or quality claims. Next, expand the held-out shadow scenarios and prepare independent blinded attraction-relevance judgments. Group emotion should be a later, separately reviewed integration stage.
